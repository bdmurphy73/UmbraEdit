# AI Policy

This is the AI policy of UmbraEdit and associated projects.

I use AI for coding where I am the product manager defining the requirements and the AI needs to develop and test. 
Final user acceptance testing is with me. When something is not they way I want it, I tell the AI what to fix. 

I forked the original novelWriter to make UmbraEdit because the novelWriter policy was to not use AI at all. 
I'm also taking the base/original idea of novelWriter and extending it into the system I want. 
That is why we all use GPL, to share and make our own version of the project.  

The ideas:

1. Write the novel. Make it simple while writing. Formatting is later.
2. Have the entire project together. Not the novel text here and metadata you need somewhere else. All together. 2.a) ePub, paperback (print pdf), audio all start with the same story text, but each needs something different. Put everything into one project (ISBNs, book blurb, search terms, genere/categories BISAC codes, cover, etc)
3. With everything together formatting and publishing is simpler
4. The goal is to write (human written) a novel, then use AI to help with everything else. At the end of the process there will be ePub, Paperback/Hard cover, audio versions in 10 languages.

The original work is still there. Things are changed when needed for the new functionality. I'm also trying to support all the languages originally supported, but I don't know how well the translations will turn out.
