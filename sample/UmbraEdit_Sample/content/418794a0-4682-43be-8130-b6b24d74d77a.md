## Chapter 2: Story Structure

This chapter demonstrates how UmbraEdit handles story structure: chapters, parts, sections, tags, and cross-references between documents.

### Scenes in This Chapter

- Scene 2.1: Chapters and Parts — how headings define story structure
- Scene 2.2: Tags and References — @tag, @char, @pov, @location, @mention
- Scene 2.3: Sections — level 4 headings within scenes

## What You'll Learn

By the end of this chapter, you will know how to:
- Use heading levels to define chapters (H2) and scenes (H3)
- Divide your manuscript into parts (H1)
- Create tags for cross-referencing
- Reference characters, POV, locations, and mentions
- Use section headings (H4) within scenes
