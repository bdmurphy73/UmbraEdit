### Old Scene

@tag: Archive.OldScene
@pov: Narrator

This is an archived scene. Archiving allows you to remove scenes from your active manuscript without deleting them. Archived scenes are excluded from word counts and exports by default.

This scene was originally part of Chapter 1 but was removed during revision. It is kept here for reference in case you want to restore parts of it later.

## Original Content

Alice followed the White Rabbit down the rabbit-hole. The rabbit-hole went straight on like a tunnel for some way, and then dipped suddenly down, so suddenly that Alice had not a moment to think about stopping herself before she found herself falling down a very deep well.

Either the well was very deep, or she fell very slowly, for she had plenty of time as she went down to look about her and to wonder what was going to happen next.

## Why Archived

This scene was archived because:
- The pacing was too slow at the beginning of the story
- The rabbit-hole scene was moved to Chapter 2
- Some of the dialogue was reused in Scene 2.1

You can restore this scene by moving it back to the Novel root folder.
