### Scene 4.2: Location References

@tag: Scene.Location
@pov: Narrator
@location: Earth, Wonderland

## Location References

The `@location:` keyword defines where a scene takes place. In this scene, the locations are Earth and Wonderland.

Earth is our familiar world. See the character sheet in the Worlds root folder for more details.

Wonderland is a fantastical location where animals talk and logic works differently. See its character sheet for more details.

## Mention References

The `@mention:` keyword is used for general mentions of people, places, or things that don't fit into the other categories. For example:

@mention: White Rabbit

The White Rabbit is a character from Alice in Wonderland who appears briefly in this example project.

## Using Location References

Location references are useful for:
- Tracking where scenes take place
- Ensuring location consistency across your manuscript
- Generating location-based reports
- World-building and setting development

## Complete Reference Example

Here is a complete example showing all reference types together:

@tag: Scene.AllRefs
@pov: Jane
@char: Jane, John
@location: Earth
@mention: White Rabbit

Jane stood by the riverbank on Earth, waiting for John. The White Rabbit had told her to meet him here at noon. She checked her pocket watch — it was already ten past.

## Where References Appear

References appear in:
- The document header/metadata (the `+++` block at the top of each file)
- The view pane when you click on a reference
- Export and build tools for generating character/location indexes
