### Scene 1.1: Text Emphasis

@tag: Scene.Emphasis
@pov: Narrator
@char: Jane

This scene demonstrates **bold** text, _italic_ text, **_bold italic_** text, and ~~strike-through~~ text.

Curly quotes are also supported: “double quotes” and ‘single quotes’. You can combine formatting inside quotes: “You can use **bold** and _italic_ text inside of quotes too.”

Here is an example from public domain text (Alice's Adventures in Wonderland by Lewis Carroll):

> Alice was beginning to get very tired of sitting by her sister on the bank, and of having nothing to do: once or twice she had peeped into the book her sister was reading, but it had no pictures or conversations in it, “and what is the use of a book,” thought Alice “without pictures or conversations?”

You can also use _nested emphasis_ like **some support for _nested_ emphasis**, though there are some known limitations with complex nesting.

==Syntax highlighting== is shown in the editor, and the export tool will handle it correctly when you build your manuscript.
