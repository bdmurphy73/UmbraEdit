## Chapter Template

Use this template when creating new chapter documents. Copy this file and replace the content with your own.

### Chapter Structure

A chapter is defined by a level two heading (##). Scenes within the chapter use level three headings (###).

### Adding Scenes

Add scene documents as child documents of this chapter, or place them at the same level below it. Both approaches produce the same output.

### Example

## Chapter Title Here

### Scene 1: Opening Scene

Your scene content goes here.

### Scene 2: Next Scene

Your next scene content goes here.
