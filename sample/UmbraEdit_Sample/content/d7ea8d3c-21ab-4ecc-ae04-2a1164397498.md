### Scene 1.2: Shortcodes

@tag: Scene.Shortcodes
@pov: Narrator

UmbraEdit supports special formatting shortcodes that go beyond basic Markdown. These are available from the Format menu and as toolbar buttons.

## Available Shortcodes

**Superscript and Subscript:**

Here we have super[sup]script[/sup] and sub[sub]script[/sub], useful for chemical formulas like H[sub]2[/sub]O or mathematical expressions like x[sup]2[/sup].

**Partial Bold:**

The [b]part[/b]ial bold shortcode ends in the middle of a word, which is useful for emphasizing just part of a term.

**Underline:**

You can [u]underline[/u] text for emphasis.

**Highlight:**

You can [m]highlight text as well[/m], which is useful for marking important passages.

**Bold and Italic Shortcodes:**

You can also use [b]bold[/b] and [i]italic[/i] as shortcodes instead of Markdown syntax.

**Example from Public Domain:**

This example combines multiple shortcodes:

> The [u]very[/u] [b]large[/b] [m]rabbit[/m] took out its [sup]own[/sup] watch.
