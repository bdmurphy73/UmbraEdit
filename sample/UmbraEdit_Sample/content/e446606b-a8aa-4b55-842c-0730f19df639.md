### Scene 3.2: Note Comments

@pov: Narrator

## Note Comments

Note comments start with `%Note.` followed by a keyword of your choosing. Like story comments, these are never included in the word count, and you can select to include or exclude them from your manuscript when exporting.

%Note.Consistency: Check that Jane's eye color is consistent with earlier scenes.
%Note.Research: Verify the historical accuracy of the pocket watch description.
%Note.Continuity: Alice should still be wearing the glove she found earlier.

% The part after the dot in your note comment is a keyword of your choosing. You can define your own keywords for tracking whatever matters to your story.

## Example Text

The White Rabbit took out its watch and looked at it, then hurried on. Alice started to her feet, for it flashed across her mind that she had never before seen a rabbit with either a waistcoat-pocket, or a watch to take out of it.

%Note.Research: Pocket watches were common in Victorian England, but a rabbit wearing one is certainly unusual.

%Note.Continuity: Make sure Alice doesn't mention the watch again in the next scene unless she remembers it.

And burning with curiosity, she ran across the field after it, and fortunately was just in time to see it pop down a large rabbit-hole under the hedge.

## Using Note Comments

Note comments are useful for:
- Tracking continuity errors
- Marking research needed
- Noting consistency issues
- Flagging scenes that need fact-checking
- Any other editorial notes
