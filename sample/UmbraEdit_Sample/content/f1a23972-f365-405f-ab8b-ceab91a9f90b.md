### Scene 4.1: POV References

@tag: Scene.POV
@pov: Jane
@char: Jane, John

## POV (Point of View)

The `@pov:` keyword defines which character's point of view the scene is told from. In this scene, the POV is Jane Smith.

Jane Smith looked across the room at John Doe. She wondered what he was thinking, but his expression gave nothing away. She took a deep breath and decided to speak first.

“John,” she said, her voice steadier than she felt, “we need to talk about what happened yesterday.”

John looked up from his book, surprised. “About the watch? I already told you, I didn’t take it.”

“I know you didn’t,” Jane replied. “But someone did. And I think I know who.”

## POV in Chapter Titles

One powerful feature of UmbraEdit is that you can use POV character tags in your chapter titles. When you build your manuscript, UmbraEdit can automatically include the POV character's name in the chapter heading, like:

**Chapter 3 — Jane's Discovery**

This is controlled in the Build Manuscript settings.

## Character References

The `@char:` keyword references characters that appear in a scene. In this scene:
- `@char: Jane` — Jane is the POV character
- `@char: John` — John also appears

See the character sheets in the Characters root folder for more details about Jane Smith and John Doe.
