## Heading Levels Define Structure

In UmbraEdit, heading levels define your story structure:

- **Level 1 (`#`)**: Parts, acts, or books
- **Level 2 (`##`)**: Chapters
- **Level 3 (`###`)**: Scenes
- **Level 4 (`####`)**: Sections within a scene

The headings define the story structure. Two `#` symbols means a chapter, and three means a scene. Adding an exclamation point changes the formatting of the heading in your manuscript. For chapters, it means they are excluded from chapter number generation.

## Chapters as Documents

This document is understood by UmbraEdit as a chapter. You can add scene documents below it either as child documents or just below it on the same level. Both result in the same output in the end, so it is a matter of preference.
