## Chapter 3: Comments and Footnotes

This chapter demonstrates how UmbraEdit handles comments and footnotes: story comments, note comments, and footnotes.

### Scenes in This Chapter

- Scene 3.1: Story Comments — %Story.keyword comments
- Scene 3.2: Note Comments — %Note.keyword comments
- Scene 3.3: Footnotes — [footnote:id] and %Footnote.id comments

## What You'll Learn

By the end of this chapter, you will know how to:
- Add story comments with custom keywords
- Add note comments with custom keywords
- Create footnotes that appear at the bottom of pages in exported documents
- Use comments for tracking consistency, resolution, and other story metadata
