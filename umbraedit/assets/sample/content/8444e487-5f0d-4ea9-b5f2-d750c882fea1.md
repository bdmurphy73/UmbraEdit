# Wonderland

@tag: Wonderland
@location: Fantasy

%Short: A fantastical location where animals talk and logic works differently.

Wonderland is a fantastical location where animals talk, logic works differently, and nothing is quite as it seems. It is inspired by Lewis Carroll's Alice's Adventures in Wonderland.

## Location Details

- **Type:** Fantasy Realm
- **Access:** Via rabbit-hole
- **Dominant Species:** Talking animals, playing cards, chess pieces
- **Notable Feature:** Logic works differently here

## Description

Wonderland is where the more surreal scenes of this example project take place. Characters who visit Wonderland find that the normal rules of reality don't apply. Animals talk, food and drink can change your size, and time doesn't always move in the same direction.
